# Cancha monetization plan (October 27, 2026)

The principle comes from the owner's direction: **useful discovery and local recognition first; money comes from organizations once Cancha sends them real attention.** Nothing here is for sale until its gate below is met. Prices are hypotheses to test, not quotes.

**What this tells us:** directories make money from **featured placement, newsletters and sponsored content**, all of it built on an audience that comes back each week (email alerts). Every competitor collects emails first.

## What Cancha already has

- Clubs collection fields `Sponsored`, `Placement Priority` and `Placement Until`, so featured placement is already technically possible and can be labeled.
- `/organizations` invites people to "ask about featured placement" through the private native form.
- A fact-checked weekly format (comparison carousels / Opportunity Drop) that can carry one clearly labeled partner slot.
- **Almost no audience yet.** 13 posts are published, and Insights covers 11 posts with no comparable groups, so there's nothing to compare. That's the real constraint.

## Stages and their gates

**Stage 0: now through November (free).**
- Weekly Opportunity Drop, free "Meet Your Local Club" features, and an email alert signup (Kit, or the native form until Kit exists).
- Measure: email subscribers, site clicks to official links, saves and shares, replies.

**Stage 1: Featured listing.** Test price $99–$149 per season, deliberately below SoccerWire's $395/year.
- **Gate:** at least 300 alert subscribers or 1,000 monthly Florida site visitors, **and** at least 3 organizations that saw inquiries they can attribute to Cancha.
- **Includes:** a labeled "Featured" badge and top placement in one city and category for the season, plus one feature post. **No guaranteed leads.**

**Stage 2: Sponsored slot in the Opportunity Drop / newsletter.** Test price $50–$150 per issue.
- **Gate:** at least 500 subscribers and a stable weekly open rate.
- It's always labeled "Sponsored", and verification rules apply exactly as for free items.

**Stage 3: Partnerships.**
- Club or event partnerships, a soccer jobs board (benchmark: $250 per listing), and co-hosted pickup or events with an operator. The ticket price isn't profit.
- Long term (owner vision): the Cancha logo on a club's shirt.

**Stage 4: Merch / gear drop.**
- **Gate:** at least 30–50 paid preorders at a healthy margin. No drinks or gels (owner note, Oct 27). Small-batch manufacturing, not dropshipping.

## Guardrails (all stages)

- Paid placement is always labeled. It never changes facts, verification, "checked on" dates or editorial rankings.
- There are no "best club" rankings for sale, no guaranteed registrations or leads, and no invented audience numbers in any pitch.
- Spending money and signing contracts is the owner's call. Claude prepares the offers and the evidence.

## The pitch, once Stage 1's gate is met (one line)

"Cancha sends Florida families who are actively comparing programs straight to your official registration page. Here's what last month's numbers were for your city and category."
