# Cancha domain and Framer badge

Official prices checked October 21, 2026. USD before tax; confirm the checkout total and any promotion before purchasing. No subscription, domain or renewal was purchased during this work.

## Lowest price with the current site structure

Cancha currently has five CMS collections: Clubs, Opportunities, Providers, Stories and Offers. Removing an empty collection alone is not enough to reach Basic's two-collection limit.

| Framer plan | Annual billing | Monthly billing | CMS limits | Fit for Cancha today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $10/month equivalent, **$120 charged yearly** | **$15/month** | 2 collections; 1,000 items | Requires a separate consolidation of three collections and their templates/automation |
| Pro | $30/month equivalent, **$360 charged yearly** | **$45/month** | 10 collections; 2,500 items | Supports the existing five-collection structure |

[Official pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing/) was checked with both billing settings. Annual Pro is the cheapest listed Framer option that preserves the current CMS structure. Monthly Pro has a smaller initial payment but costs $540 across twelve months, plus the domain.

Framer advertises an eligible custom domain free for the first year with a first yearly site subscription, through Hover. Availability, eligible names/TLDs and discounts are checked during redemption; this does not mean every domain or premium name is free. It renews at the registrar's standard rate after that year. Framer says redeeming this domain offer forfeits the site-plan refund option. See [official domains information](https://www.framer.com/domains/).

The Made in Framer badge can be removed through a paid site plan and custom-domain setup, followed by republishing. Do not use CSS to obscure it. See [Framer's badge instructions](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/how-do-i-remove-the-made-in-framer-badge-from-my-website/).

## Ways to spend less

**Basic after consolidation:** keep Clubs and Opportunities in CMS; move the small Providers/Stories directories and currently empty Offers collection to reviewed repository data while preserving their URLs, forms and update workflow. That is a real migration, not a plan-toggle shortcut. It needs a backup, template/binding audit and route verification before deleting collections. The 1,000-item limit must also fit the intended directory growth. No collections have been removed.

**Different hosting:** static hosting can reduce the recurring hosting bill, but replacing Framer means migrating the site, CMS publishing, forms and automation. It does not produce the same operational setup simply by changing DNS. Price that work separately before cancelling anything.

An independently registered domain is optional. [Cloudflare Registrar](https://www.cloudflare.com/domains/) sells at registry cost; other registrars have different first-year and renewal rates. Compare the *renewal* price for the exact name, not only a first-year offer. No exact registrar quote is claimed without a chosen name/TLD.

## Connecting the domain securely

1. Choose the domain, or identify one already owned. The domain name is public; no password, API key or registrar login belongs in chat.
2. The owner buys/upgrades the chosen site plan in Framer. Check billing frequency and total before paying.
3. Open Cancha → Site settings → Domains → Connect a domain. Copy the exact DNS records Framer currently shows.
4. In the registrar's DNS screen, add only those records for the web hostnames. Preserve mail records (MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC); do not replace the whole DNS zone or nameservers unnecessarily.
5. Wait for Framer to verify DNS and HTTPS, set the preferred domain, and republish. Check the apex/www redirect, club and opportunity routes, form delivery and badge removal.
6. Update the Instagram bio and campaign destination after the new HTTPS domain works. Preserve tracking parameters. The existing framer.app site should not be disabled before these checks pass.

[Official connection guide](https://www.framer.com/help/articles/how-to-connect-a-custom-domain/). DNS values are intentionally taken from the live Framer settings rather than hard-coded here, because provider instructions can change.

The chosen domain and owner billing action remain outstanding. No purchase or account access is required for directory growth and design work.
