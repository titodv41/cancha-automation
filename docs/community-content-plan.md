# Community-first Instagram

Owner direction, October 21, 2026: keep the clean style of the last two posts; favor community participation over promotion. Existing authorization remains one eligible feed post daily around 6 PM America/New_York. GitHub can start late. A Draft outside the eight-hour scheduling window is normal; it is not an already scheduled post. No Stories or automatic Reels are enabled.

## What changed

- Community questions now receive priority until four posts in the calendar week's scheduled/published mix cover that topic, when eligible content is available. This is a target, not a claim that seven good posts always exist.
- Three earlier promotional/product-guide drafts and two empty-field/stadium photo drafts are excluded from new automatic selection. Published posts and their receipts are preserved.
- Three inspected community assets are delivered by the `refresh-community` workflow action: a welcoming-club question, a returning-player question and a credited Florida field photo asking about regular games. They use the same source/creative validation, freshness and one-post-per-day checks as the existing system.
- The daily preparation pool includes a parent-advice discussion and can add current source-backed club/place features. Repeat photos/subjects are excluded for 30 days; weak, stale or exhausted content is skipped.

Aim for four community conversations/recommendations, two useful guides/local profiles and one directory demonstration per week. Do not fill a quota with seven versions of the same question. Alternate image, map, profile and graphic formats. Current usable photography is limited: do not conceal that limit with repeated crops. Ask actual organizers for permitted media before depicting their people or events.

## What people can contribute

Ask for one concrete contribution at a time: club + city; a public venue; a public organizer page; a question a parent wishes they had asked. Avoid collecting children's names, precise personal locations, private group invitations or phone numbers in public comments. Suggestions are research leads, not verified listings or scheduled pickup games.

A founder can acknowledge thoughtful replies and ask a relevant follow-up. This setup does **not** read/reply to Instagram comments or Facebook groups automatically. No fake comments, testimonials, members, news or games. Facebook group distribution requires an administrator's rules/permission and an actual helpful reply; draft templates are in `content/growth/`.
