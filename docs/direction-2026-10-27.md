# Cancha direction — October 27, 2026 (owner)

**Useful discovery + recognition for local soccer + real relationships.** Start with Orlando, Tampa Bay and Miami/South Florida. No Gainesville-area outreach for now.

## Positioning
Cancha helps people discover soccer opportunities and introduces the people building soccer in Florida. It needs both of these:
- **Useful information:** camps, training, clubs, pickup and opportunities.
- **Local stories:** who runs these programs, how they started and what they're trying to build.

## Content
- **Comparison carousels and the weekly "Opportunity Drop".** Each one shows dates, eligibility, price and extra costs, location, the official registration link and when we checked. Unverified details are never published. The pipeline is `content/instagram/comparison-carousels.json`, and every item is re-checked against its organizer page before posting.
- **"Meet Your Local Club" / "Meet Your Local Coach" · <city>.** Organizations send short phone videos answering:
  1. Who are you, and how did this start?
  2. Who do you serve?
  3. What are your goals?
  4. What should someone know before joining?

  We turn the clips into a Reel, a carousel and a linked directory profile. Each feature ends with practical details: where they train, who can join and how to contact them. We need permission before publishing their footage or quotes. The first features are free, because we're building relationships and learning what helps.
- **Cadence test:** about 4 useful feed posts a week, mixing comparisons, opportunity updates, local stories and parent/player guides. Track saves, shares, website clicks, email replies and useful inquiries. The cadence is a test, not a rule.

## Outreach
- 10 individual research emails went out on Oct 27 from the owner's Gmail to programs in Orlando, Tampa Bay and South Florida. The list is kept privately; never send duplicates.
- Conversations happen by email first. When someone replies positively, suggest a feature. There are no sales pitches and no automated outreach.

## Immediate objectives
Move quickly on the domain, CMS migration, core camp pages and Search Console, with content and outreach running alongside. We don't need every page finished before launch.

## Long-term vision (not a promise)
Cancha becomes recognizable at local fields, and eventually appears as a sponsor on a club's shirt.

## Brand language (still exploring, not final)
- Tagline options: "CANCHA — Passion that grows" (original) or "CANCHA — Where soccer grows".
- Website CTA option: "Find Your Game".
