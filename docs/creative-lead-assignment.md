## Research before making another template

Inspect a small set of current sports publishers, local club accounts and useful discovery products. Save exact links/screenshots and explain which specific technique is worth testing. Distinguish evidence of performance from a style preference. Do not claim a post converted or retained viewers from its appearance or likes alone.

Focus the work on one useful audience: a Florida player, parent or local fan. A daily story should say something they can use, share with their club, or discuss. A profile promotion, a club result and a community guide need different treatments. Each carousel swipe must add information and a distinct relevant photograph or original explanatory visual.

## Brand and composition

Use the actual brand assets from `content/instagram/photo-editorial.json`:

- Italic Barlow Condensed display identity; bold upright supporting type. Make deliberate hierarchy and spacing choices, with actual phone previews. Do not introduce an unrelated body family without a reason and a comparison.
- Forest `#103f32`, cream `#f5f3e9`, lime `#d7ef86`, orange `#f55b32`.
- Real subject media, clear blocks, short labels and purposeful graphic shapes. No fake players, partners, membership figures, synthetic club photographs or filler stock loops.
- One clear point per slide. Keep supporting copy short enough to read on a 390-pixel phone. Text and photos have separate measured regions. Logos remain visible and correctly proportioned.
- Narration explains more than the heading. Open with a concrete change or useful question and end on a meaningful fact or action. Do not force an unrelated closing question. Retain source evidence outside the artwork.

## Required outputs

1. Three rendered concept thumbnails with a sentence explaining each direction. Show actual media and representative typography, rather than empty wireframes.
2. One complete three-slide 1080×1350 carousel as PNG/JPEG and a combined PDF, with three image descriptions and one concise caption. Provide source links separately.
3. A 12–20-second spoken script and a frame/shot plan using actual subject footage, meaningful animation and the selected Chris voice. Do not request new TTS or change voice limits. A visual storyboard can be produced without spending narration credits.
4. One implemented improvement to a homepage or discovery section on a separate branch, with 390- and 1440-pixel previews. Preserve routes, filters, CMS records, branding and the private submission forms.
5. One distribution experiment: audience, useful promise, proposed local-club sharing angle, tracked destination and the observable result to measure. Do not contact anyone or buy ads as part of this assignment.

## Review criteria

Score each of these with visible examples rather than a generic self-approval:

- Can a person identify the specific club/topic and the useful fact quickly?
- Does every slide add value and keep text away from the photograph?
- Does the work visibly belong to Cancha while varying from the last few posts?
- Does the caption sound natural and remain supported by the source?
- Is the phone version readable, with no clipping, broken image, tiny essential text or weak contrast?
- Does the website change make a search or next action easier?

Record what changed after the first critique. Style scores are judgments, not engagement evidence. Keep source/byte/layout checks alongside this review; they establish correctness but do not prove taste or growth.
