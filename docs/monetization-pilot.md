# Cancha revenue experiments

Research checked October 21, 2026. These are proposed tests, not products currently for sale. No payment service, subscription, ad spend or paid placement was activated.

## Start with a small featured-placement pilot

Keep accurate basic club profiles, corrections, search and submissions free. First test whether a local club values measurable visibility in a relevant search. A paid position must say **Sponsored placement**, respect city/age/type filters, have an end date and never imply better coaching or Cancha certification. A club should not have to pay to correct its information.

Use the existing **Featured placement enquiry** option on `/organizations`; submissions go privately to the configured email. The footer links to it. The query string alone does not guarantee the option is preselected, so the club should choose it in the form. No new destination is needed.

Proposed first experiment: one city, at most three clubs, 30 days, manual onboarding and a simple results report. Test willingness to pay at **$29 and $59 for a month** in founder conversations. These are price hypotheses, not researched market rates or a published offer. Do not promise leads, reach, registrations or renewal. Only set a price after showing an actual traffic baseline and agreeing the deliverables.

Before charging: obtain three explicit expressions of interest; confirm the placement and disclosure work on phones; record two weeks of relevant non-QA visits; agree the start/end dates and cancellation/refund terms. If traffic is too small to report useful exposure, run a disclosed complimentary trial first. Do not bill for an empty audience.

## Order of experiments

| Model | Test | Evidence to continue | Main constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Featured club profiles | Three local pilots with an expiry and Sponsored label | Clubs understand the report and at least two want another month | Location-specific traffic must exist first |
| Coach/service profiles | Free verified contact profiles; interview five actual providers about paid visibility | Repeated requests for promotion and relevant enquiries | Verify identity and stated credentials; payment cannot buy a verification claim |
| Local sponsor | One clearly labeled city guide supported by a relevant business | A useful guide, a willing sponsor and measurable visits | Obtain media rights and agree deliverables before accepting money |
| Club promotion service | A fixed, manual package using organizer-supplied photos and checked information | Two clubs pay for the work and can explain its value | Founder time; avoid building software before demand repeats |
| Display ads | Revisit after sustained useful traffic | Revenue estimate exceeds the design/privacy/maintenance cost | Low traffic makes it a weak first revenue test |
| Booking/registration commission | Consider only after people repeatedly ask to book through Cancha | Actual transaction demand | Refunds, disputes, support and payment compliance add ongoing work |

The present site is a discovery directory; it does not handle registration payments or guarantee a team place. Keep it that way during the first visibility experiment.

## Simple economics, clearly hypothetical

At $29, a US domestic card payment at Stripe's listed standard rate of 2.9% + $0.30 would leave about **$27.86**, before tax, refunds, other fees and founder time. At $59 it would leave about **$56.99**. Ten $29 placements would be $290 gross, not profit. This arithmetic does not establish demand.

For display ads, revenue is impressions / 1,000 × RPM. For illustration only, 10,000 monetized impressions at an assumed $5 RPM would be $50. Actual RPM, fill, eligibility and traffic are unknown; this is not an AdSense quote or forecast.

OpenSports currently lists event-management plans at $30/month, $70/month and $750+/month, with 3% platform fees on applicable transactions, excluding Stripe fees. That is an operations product with payments, memberships and leagues. Its prices are useful context for what clubs already buy, **not a comparable price for a Cancha directory position**.

## Weekly founder scorecard

Record: relevant city-directory visits, profile views, official-site link clicks if separately measurable, listing/update submissions, real promotion enquiries, pilot price quoted, accepted/declined and reason. Existing Framer reports contain aggregate pageviews/clicks/form events and campaign attribution. Generic clicks are not automatically organizer contact clicks, and visits are not registrations. Owner/QA visits contaminate early counts. Instagram publishing access does not establish Insights access.

Do not send outreach automatically. A short future conversation prompt: “We help people find soccer in [city]. What do you currently use to attract players? Would a labeled local feature with an honest traffic report be useful?” Capture the response before proposing a paid product.
