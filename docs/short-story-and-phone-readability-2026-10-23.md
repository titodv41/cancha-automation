## Research applied

- [YouTube Shorts discussion with the product lead and Jenny Hoyos, January 28, 2025](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/): prioritize the opening second and a concise mini-story. This is creator guidance, not evidence of a guaranteed Instagram result.
