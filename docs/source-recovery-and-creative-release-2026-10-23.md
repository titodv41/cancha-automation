## Reusable creative workflow

1. Choose a specific audience question and check official facts. Record supporting text/expiry in `config/local-editorial-sources.json`. Write a spoken explanation separately from the shorter screen copy; keep availability, proposed developments and existing clubs distinct.
2. Put slide copy, theme, exact photos and crop/position in `content/instagram/photo-editorial.json`. Each slide must use a different original subject photo. Two unstable Orlando CDN images have exact, checksum-verified mirrors of their reviewed originals; source identity and source freshness checks still apply. New crops or mirrors never count as new photos.
3. Render with `npm run content:photos`; inspect every slide at full and phone size. The renderer checks current source facts, image identity/resolution, font loading and text bounds. Use `npm run content:photos -- --upload` only after review.
4. Product posts live in `content/instagram/product-editorial.json`. Run `npm run content:products` to capture the real public directory and render, inspect it, then append `-- --upload`. Only the two curated IDs with fresh screenshot identity, public source and matching hosted image bytes can enter the photo-first exception.
5. Share review files with `node scripts/share-photo-editorial.mjs --include-products`. Public links go into `content/instagram/photo-editorial-preview-links.json`; private evidence stays under ignored `private-data/` and rebuildable previews stay ignored.
6. Commit/push reviewed content. Dispatch `github-instagram.yml` with `refresh-photo-stories` and `refresh-product-posts`. The carousel refresh preserves untouched future standing approvals and their slots, and protects dispatched rows and receipts. The product refresh updates Drafts only. Inspect the resulting private status artifact.
7. New voice generation remains a manual, capped `premium-voice.yml` → `free-audition`. Download the private successful artifact. Use `npm run content:narrative -- --voice PATH.mp3 --timing PATH.json`, then `npm run content:narrative-share -- previews/narrative-story/report-chris.json` (or Jessica). The edit follows real provider sentence/word times; no synthetic guessed duration or rejected local-voice fallback. Renderer/source/audio hashes bind the preview report. Re-render visuals using the cached audio without spending more TTS credits.

The standing plan keeps 12:30 PM and 6 PM New York slots, at least four actual hours apart. It reserves a daily photo carousel before a product post; product posts are capped at two per week and their website/screenshot artwork is rechecked. Reels remain held while the owner reviews the revised video direction. No old publication receipt is overwritten and uncertain publication is never automatically retried.

This is a reusable production and publishing system, not an unattended art director. Automatic discovery/publication operates on supported sources and eligible prepared content. A finite seven-post bank cannot produce new original stories indefinitely. No audience growth or improved engagement is claimed from rendering alone. Instagram insights still need the appropriate account permission before performance learning can be measured.

For another project, replace the audience/brand/source manifests, fonts, media and account configuration. Keep its credential store, voice budget, submissions and publication state separate. Preserve source checks, private backups, media identity, publication receipts and budget caps.
