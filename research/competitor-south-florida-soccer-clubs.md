# Competitor: South Florida Soccer Clubs (southfloridasoccerclubs.com)

Checked Nov 2, 2026 by Claude against the live pages. These are public offers and self-reported claims, not proof of sales or traffic.

## Verified on their pages
- **Prices** (sfsc-updates-advertising): search-area listing $109/mo; banner $229/mo; bundle $429/mo; featured club logo $67.50/mo; Player ID $22.50/mo; discounts for 6/12-month terms. The /clubs form says the upgrade (logo, links, info) is $125/mo, which conflicts with the $109 price.
- **Classifieds:** $25 per listing for 30 days, auto-renewing, cancelled by email. Personal use only.
- **ROI pitch:** "one player at $150/mo × 10 months = $1,500/year covers a $109 listing." It uses gross fees, not margin.
- **Traffic claim:** "21,000 unique visitors per month organically" (advertise-with-us). Self-reported and undated on the page; not independently verified.
- **Gaps:** Player ID and Soccer Fields/Pick-Up pages both say "Under Construction" with nothing listed. The Miami-Dade tryouts page is mostly a submission form. A Spanish page exists (so bilingual is not unique to us).
- **Strengths:** about 60 long parent guides (SEO traffic), a free parents' community (SPA: pickup, watch parties), and a founder with 21 years as a South Florida soccer parent and coach.

## What we take
1. Sell on the buyer's economics, but honestly: show tracked registration/contact clicks, and use the buyer's margin, not gross fees.
2. Guides that answer real decisions, linked to live listings.
3. Keep basic listings free; paid placement is always labelled and never buys "verified".

## Where we compete
- **Places to play** (fields, pickup, adult leagues), where their pages are empty and we already list parks and pickup groups.
- **Checked details:** dates, ages and gender, total cost notes, registration status, last-checked date, a direct link.
- **Visual Instagram and Reels, plus local stories.**

Positioning to test: "Find your next place to play in South Florida, with the details checked."
