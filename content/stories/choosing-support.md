# Choose support that fits your next step

The right support starts with a clear goal. Are you improving a skill, preparing for a demanding season, exploring college options or organizing your highlights? Write down the outcome you want before comparing services.

## Ask about fit
Ask which ages and experience levels a provider works with, how sessions are delivered, and what a typical plan looks like. Confirm whether the service is local, remote or a combination, and whether the language and schedule work for you.

## Ask about qualifications
Ask for the qualifications relevant to the service and how you can verify them. Distinguish a provider’s own description from a recorded credential check. A directory profile or reviewed credential does not guarantee results.

For nutrition, mental health or medical concerns, confirm the professional’s scope and qualifications directly. Mental performance coaching and clinical care are different services.

## Understand the agreement
Confirm the full price, session length, cancellation policy and what is included. Ask how progress is discussed. Avoid treating a promise of selection, scholarships or a guaranteed outcome as evidence of quality.

If the service involves a minor, include a parent or guardian in decisions and clarify safeguarding, communication and supervision arrangements.
