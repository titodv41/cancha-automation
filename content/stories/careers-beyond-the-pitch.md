# Your next role in soccer can be beyond the pitch

Playing is one way to be part of soccer. Clubs, events and leagues also rely on people who communicate clearly, organize operations, support fans and manage information.

## Start with work you can show
Look for the overlap between your interests and a role’s actual responsibilities. A concise writing sample, an organized event plan or a project you completed can help you explain your skills. Use your own work and describe your contribution honestly.

## Read the role, not just the title
Check required experience, working hours, location and work authorization. Match days may involve evenings and weekends. An internship title does not tell you whether a role is paid or whether college credit is required—read the posting and ask the employer.

## Make the next step specific
Choose a role you can explain a real interest in. Tailor your application to its requirements, keep track of the deadline and apply through the employer’s current link. If a posting is closed, look for another current role instead of assuming an old application link still works.

No path guarantees a job. A useful starting point is a clear example of what you can contribute and a thoughtful application to a role that is still accepting candidates.
