# Five questions before you register

An opportunity can sound right and still need a closer look. Before you spend money or commit your time, ask these five questions.

## 1. Is this the organizer’s current page?
Start with the club, school, employer or event organizer’s own website. Check that the season or event year matches. A directory listing, working image or familiar logo does not prove that registration is still open.

## 2. Am I eligible?
Read the exact age, grade, birth-date, experience and location requirements. A broad directory tag such as “All Ages” is not an eligibility rule. If the page contradicts itself, ask the organizer before paying.

## 3. What is the full commitment?
Check dates, travel, fees, equipment and cancellation terms. For jobs and internships, ask about hours, compensation and any college-credit arrangements. Do not assume that a price note includes every fee.

## 4. Is there still a place?
A visible registration button can lead to a waitlist, evaluation request or application. Ask whether places remain and what acceptance means. Save the organizer’s reply and your confirmation.

## 5. What happens next?
Know who will contact you, how you will receive instructions and where to ask questions. Keep a copy of your registration and the terms you accepted.
