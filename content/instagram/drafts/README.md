# Draft scripts (owner, Nov 17)

Claude makes Reel drafts from real footage; the owner reviews them, gets any club or footage OK, and says when to post.
Each `<name>.json` here holds a draft's narration script (`id: draft-...`, `script`, `narrationApprovedOn`).
`draft-voice.yml` turns it into Chris's narration under the capped Starter plan. Nothing here is posted: a draft goes
to the queue only after the owner's go, through the normal creator-cut path with its footage recorded in
`config/clip-permissions.json`.
