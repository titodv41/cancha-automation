# Cancha video style (owner, Nov 12, 2026)

The house rules for every Reel. Read before writing a script or a spec. When the owner reacts to a draft, add the lesson here.

## What the owner wants
- **Simple, minimal, on brand.** Forest `#103f32`, cream `#f5f3e9`, lime `#d7ef86`, orange `#f55b32`. Barlow Condensed (italic display, upright 700 for support). The real logo on every frame.
- **Never "vibe coded" or AI-looking.** No generic stock, no fake people, no flashy effects. Real footage, real screens, real faces.
- **Voice and picture move together.** Each on-screen line starts on its cue word (`beats[].cue`) and lands on a cut. The picture changes when the line changes. Tests enforce this (`test/cue-timeline.test.mjs`).
- **Calm transitions.** 0.4 s crossfades (`FADE=12`), a slow push on footage. No hard jump cuts between unrelated scenes, and no whip pans or glitch effects.
- **A reason to follow.** Every Reel belongs to a named series and ends with what the series gives you, e.g. "New breakdowns every week".

## Formats that work (references the owner sent)
1. **Interview split (ESPN "SC Shorts" style).**
   - Top half: a person talking, with big two-line subtitles in their own words.
   - Bottom half: a still brand card with a photo of the people involved and the series mark.
   - The hook is a strong quote. It works because a real person says something surprising.
   - For Cancha: local players, coaches and club owners. The owner gets the permission and the footage.
2. **Data carousel (Soccer Insider on LinkedIn).**
   - One big number per slide (e.g. "$205M").
   - A short claim under it that gives the number meaning.
   - A real stadium photo, dimmed.
   - Sources in small print on every slide, and the brand at the bottom.
   - Our Brief carousels already follow this. Keep the big number and the dimmed real photo.
3. **The Messi free-kick physics Reel (our best so far, about 100 likes).**
   - A huge name and a question people actually wonder about.
   - Explained in under a minute with the creator's own graphics.

## Topics, in order
1. **Soccer business**, explained with numbers: fees, valuations, how clubs make money, the stadium.
   - Each beat answers a question a fan would ask, and every number is sourced in the caption.
2. **Miami and South Florida clubs and moments.**
   - Name the club and send one collab invite to that club. When the Tampa Bay Rowdies reshared our post, we got our first likes.
3. **How the pros play.** One idea, five steps, shown with real footage.
4. **The app.** Real screens only, with example content labeled.

## Script rules
- One story per Reel. The narration explains it; it doesn't read the slide text.
- Open on a name, a number or a face in the first second. Keep the hook to three short lines on screen.
- Use short sentences and plain words. Opinions go only in the last line. End with one call to action.
- Aim for 25–45 s. Go up to 60 s only when the story earns it.
- Never invent facts, partners, results or urgency. Credit the creator on screen the whole time and in the caption.

## Distribution (each Reel)
- Send one collab invite: the featured club, or the creator who cleared the footage.
- In the first hour, the owner and friends share it to stories and send it in Miami soccer chats. That is a person doing it, never automation.
