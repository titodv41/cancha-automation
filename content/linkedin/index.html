About Cancha

Cancha is a soccer discovery platform and brand starting in Florida. It helps players and parents find clubs, academies, camps, coaches, development programs and places to play. The initial focus is Orlando and the surrounding area, Tampa Bay, and Miami/South Florida. Alongside practical program information, Cancha is developing Meet Your Local Club and Meet Your Local Coach: short features about the people behind local soccer, who they serve and what families should know before joining. Founded by soccer coach and former college player Jose Del Vecchio, Cancha is in its early stages. The directory is growing, and the project is reaching out to local operators as it prepares for a fuller launch. Follow for Florida soccer opportunities, useful guides and updates from the people building the local game.

2. The people behind local soccer

Cancha Company Page · Local club, academy, camp and coaching operators; parents comparing programs

Post copy
Knowing who runs a program can help a family understand what joining might look like. Cancha is developing Meet Your Local Club and Meet Your Local Coach, two short feature formats about the people behind Florida soccer. The idea is to hear from coaches and organizers in their own words: how their programs started, who they work with and what they want players to learn. Those stories will sit alongside useful information about the programs themselves. The first focus is Orlando, Tampa Bay and Miami/South Florida. Coaches and operators: what would you want a family to know before their first session?
