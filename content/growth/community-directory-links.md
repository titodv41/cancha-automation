# Community distribution drafts — not sent

Use only where a group admin permits useful local resource replies. Read the actual request first, avoid repeat promotional replies and never harvest or message the member list.

## A parent looking for recreational soccer near Tampa

"What part of Tampa are you near? Here are recreational club profiles around 33602 for a 12-year-old: [browse options](https://pioneering-friday-005497.framer.app/clubs?near=33602&playerAge=12&play=Recreational&utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=florida_discovery&utm_content=parent_reply). The profiles link to the clubs—check the right age group, current fees and spaces directly. If you share a closer ZIP, the search can be narrowed."

Use the requester's actual voluntarily supplied area/age; this example is not a claim that a specific child is eligible or that registration is open.

## Someone seeking an organized pickup game

"Here are [pickup organizers around Orlando](https://pioneering-friday-005497.framer.app/clubs?near=32801&playerAge=18&activity=Pickup+groups&utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=florida_discovery&utm_content=pickup_reply). Check the organizer's next game, booking rules and age requirements. A park listing alone doesn't mean a game is happening there."

## Ask an admin before a directory/resource post

"I’m building Cancha, a Florida soccer directory for clubs, fields, training and pickup. Would a local resource post be useful for this group? I can link directly to the relevant area and invite organizers to correct or submit their own public information. I’ll follow your posting rules."

## Invite an organizer to submit their own group

"If you'd like your pickup group or community considered for Cancha, [send its public information here](https://pioneering-friday-005497.framer.app/organizations). Choose ‘Pickup group or community’. Include the public organizer/group link, area, ages, level, booking method and how venue access is arranged. Contact details stay private and submissions are reviewed before publication."

No contact export, automatic group post, DM, Meta subscription or advertising spend is part of these drafts. An admin's permission to post a resource is separate from an organizer's permission to republish group information or photos.
