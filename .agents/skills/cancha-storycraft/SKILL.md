Read `skills/soccer-motion/SKILL.md` and `docs/storycraft-workflow.md`. This project adapter applies selected official Remotion guidance pinned in `references.json`; consult those exact upstream references for timing, text measurement, local fonts and overlapping scenes. It does not install a second publishing service or authorize paid narration.

1. Begin with one useful question and one subject. Establish what changed, the actual date, place and consequence from current official sources. Distinguish an expansion team from an existing club, a general manager from a coach, and a proposed event from a confirmed one. Register the exact supporting text before rendering.
2. Write a short spoken script and a visual plan together. Each scene must answer a different part of the same question. Remove attribution filler such as “reported by”; retain exact sources in editorial evidence/caption links. Never invent figures to make a number animation.
3. Animate meaning. Reveal the actual season as staggered digits; trace a real route only with verified geography; use a venue pin for a named ground; use a comparison when there are two different statuses. Do not animate arbitrary counters, fake attendance or made-up roster names. Never imply women’s match footage shows an unlaunched men’s team.
4. Preserve Cancha’s italic Barlow Condensed display font. Use Manrope 650–750 for short supporting text, with a minimum 29 px on a 1080 px carousel or 34 px on a 1080×1920 video. Use sentence case for explanations. Keep one headline and one useful supporting sentence per scene. Leave the Reel interface and bottom caption areas clear.
5. Use deterministic frame-driven easing and staggered reveals, with short overlapping scene transitions. Keep real photos/video visible. Fonts must finish loading before measurements and rendering. Check actual text bounds at stable frames and the middle of transitions. Do not shrink everything to fit an overlong script.
6. Use actual contributor speech when available. Free Pocket preset auditions are allowed under the recorded licenses, but their sound is not approved by a transcript or file check. ElevenLabs Free does not grant a commercial license; do not assume pre-revenue brand promotion is excluded. Paid generation remains disabled. Make a music-only version available when synthetic narration has not been accepted.
7. Render a public MP4 for this owner’s established chat-review workflow; an inaccessible local Studio link is not a delivered preview. Inspect each scene and transition, check a phone-sized playback, and audit the final encoded file’s duration, size, loudness and codec. Keep source/renderer/font hashes in the report.
8. Keep new Reels preview-only under the existing hold. Update only future untouched carousel drafts/standing approvals through the supported refresh action. Preserve published and uncertain receipts. Share public HTTPS previews and state exactly what was imported, scheduled or published.

For reuse with another project, replace the brand, subject registry, media authorization and account configuration. Do not copy Cancha credentials or publishing history.
